Included Days

Which days of the week are active schedule days for this project. Unchecked days are greyed out and unselectable in The Bridge, can't be picked when moving/rescheduling an activity in Schedule, and are skipped by Bridge's automatic downstream-move cascade.

Blackout Dates

Specific one-off dates that aren't active schedule days — holidays, planned shutdowns, etc. — on top of the recurring Included Days pattern above.

Status Refresh Schedule

Times of day (in the chosen time zone) at which every occupied row's Status column is fully repopulated, not just the rolling 7-day window already kept current in the background. Requires at least one browser tab with this project open at that time — there is no server-side schedule behind this.

Dashboard Help & Training

Turns commissioning data into an executive-readable progress picture.

📊 CxAlloy (verified) 🗓️ LaunchPad (planned) 📈 Planned vs. Actual

It reads directly from the commissioning software of record (CxAlloy) – issues, checklists, functional tests, equipment, people and companies – and pairs it with planned QCx activity data from LaunchPad, so the planned and the verified side of the job sit in one view.

Because both sides are present, the dashboard reports progress against schedule rather than in isolation: completion by trade and by contractor, aging and escalation, test pass rates, and equipment-level readiness. Every metric filters by discipline, trade partner, status and building phase, so the same dashboard serves a project executive looking at the whole job and a foreman looking only at their own scope.

Discipline Trade Partner Status Building Phase

The intent is not another reporting screen. It takes data the team already trusts, presents it in an interface built to drive action rather than to be read, and uses planned-versus-actual to make the picture predictive instead of reactive.

The client-facing build ships as a self-contained static bundle that drops into an existing web app behind the customer's own login.
